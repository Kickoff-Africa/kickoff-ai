FEDERAL AIRPORTS AUTHORITY OF NIGERIA (FAAN)
PASSENGER FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
Customer Service Department

Welcome FAAN Passenger Information and Customer Support.

At the Federal Airports Authority of Nigeria (FAAN), we are committed to providing a safe, secure, comfortable and seamless airport experience.

This Frequently Asked Questions section provides answers to common questions about airport activities, airline operations, passenger services, airport facilities, safety, security and complaints.

SECTION 1: GENERAL AIRPORT INFORMATION

1. What is FAAN?
The Federal Airports Authority of Nigeria (FAAN) is the government agency responsible for the management and operation of designated federal airports in Nigeria. Its responsibilities include airport infrastructure, terminal facilities, passenger services, airport operations and the provision of a safe and efficient airport environment.

2. Which airports are managed by FAAN?
FAAN manages several federal airports across Nigeria, including:
- Murtala Muhammed International Airport, Lagos.
- Nnamdi Azikiwe International Airport, Abuja.
- Mallam Aminu Kano International Airport, Kano.
- Port Harcourt International Airport.
- Akanu Ibiam International Airport, Enugu.
- Sam Mbakwe International Cargo Airport, Owerri.
- Benin Airport and other designated federal airports.

3. What time does the airport open?
Airport operating hours vary depending on the airport and flight operations. Passengers should confirm their flight schedules with their airlines and check the relevant airport information before travelling.

4. How early should I arrive at the airport before my flight?
Passengers are advised to arrive:
- At least 2 hours before domestic flights.
- At least 3 hours before international flights.
However, passengers should follow their airlines' specific check-in requirements.

5. Where can I get information about flight arrivals and departures?
Passengers can obtain flight information from:
- The airline's official website or mobile application.
- Airport flight information display screens.
- Airline customer service desks.
- Official airport information channels, FAAN Customer Service.

6. Can I enter the airport if I am not travelling?
Yes. Non-travelling visitors may access designated public areas, subject to airport security procedures and applicable access restrictions. Access to restricted areas is prohibited without proper authorisation.

7. Where can I make enquiries when I arrive at the airport?
Passengers can approach the airport information desk or customer service personnel for assistance and directions.

8. Where can I find my Airline?
Due to the construction of the terminal building the airlines are divided into two terminals (Terminal 2 and the temporary terminal).
Terminal 2 has the following airlines: British Airways, Delta Airlines, Emirates, Turkish, Qatar, Air Peace, Ibom Air, Air Cote d'Ivoire, South African, Lufthansa, United, Kenya Airways, Rwanda Air, Royal Air Maroc, TAAG Angola, Air Uganda, Virgin Atlantic, AWA, Asky, Egypt Air.
For the Temporary Terminal, the departing airlines are: ValuJet, Air Gabon, Ethiopian, KLM and Air France.

SECTION 2: AIRLINE SERVICES AND FLIGHT INFORMATION

8. How do I book a flight?
Passengers can book flights directly through an airline's official website, mobile application, authorised travel agents or airline ticket offices.

9. How do I confirm whether my flight is delayed or cancelled?
Contact your airline directly or check its official communication channels. Airlines are responsible for notifying passengers about flight delays, cancellations and schedule changes.

10. What should I do if my flight is cancelled?
Contact your airline immediately for information about:
- Rebooking or alternative flights.
- Refund eligibility.
- Accommodation or other assistance, where applicable.
- Further travel arrangements.

11. Who is responsible for refunding my flight ticket?
The airline or authorised ticketing agent through which the ticket was purchased is responsible for processing applicable refunds, subject to the airline's conditions and relevant aviation regulations.

12. What should I do if I miss my flight?
Contact your airline's customer service or ticketing office immediately to discuss rebooking options and any applicable charges.

13. Can FAAN change my flight or assist with airline ticket bookings?
No. FAAN does not sell airline tickets, change flight schedules, issue boarding passes or determine airline fares. These services are handled by the respective airlines.

14. Who is responsible for flight delays?
The airline is responsible for managing its flight operations and communicating flight delays to passengers. Passengers who experience delays should seek clarification and assistance from the airline.

15. How can I contact an airline at the airport?
Passengers should approach the airline's check-in counter, customer service desk or authorised representative within the terminal.

16. Who regulates airline operations in Nigeria?
The Nigerian Civil Aviation Authority (NCAA) is responsible for the regulation and oversight of civil aviation activities in Nigeria.

SECTION 3: CHECK-IN, BOARDING AND DEPARTURE PROCEDURES

17. Where do I check in for my flight?
Proceed to the check-in counters designated for your airline. Flight information display screens within the terminal indicate the relevant counters.

18. What documents do I need for domestic flights and where is Ibom Air taking off?
Passengers should present a valid means of identification and any other documents required by their airline. Also know your terminal.
Airlines flying from MM1 and MM2 (Murtala Muhammed Airport, Lagos):
MM1 (GAT): Air Peace, Arik, Eagle Nigeria, Green Africa, Overland.
MM2: Air Peace, Ibom Air, United, Arik, Aero, ValuJet, Max Air, Rano Air, Enugu Air.

19. What documents are required for international flights?
International passengers generally require:
- A valid international passport.
- A valid visa or entry permit, where required.
- Flight ticket or booking confirmation.
- Other travel documents required by the destination country.

20. Where do I go after checking in?
After checking in, proceed to the designated security screening area and follow the signs to immigration (for international flights), departure lounges and boarding gates, as applicable.

21. Why are passengers subjected to security screening?
Security screening is conducted to protect passengers, airport users, aircraft and airport facilities. All passengers and their belongings must undergo the prescribed security checks.

22. Can I refuse to undergo airport security screening?
No. Security screening is a mandatory airport security requirement. Passengers must comply with lawful screening procedures.

23. Why are some items not allowed through security screening?
Certain items may pose safety or security risks. Prohibited and restricted items are subject to applicable aviation security regulations and screening requirements.

24. What should I do if my personal belongings are damaged or mishandled during screening?
Passengers should immediately report the incident to the security agency or personnel responsible for the screening and request that the incident be documented. They may also submit a complaint through FAAN Customer Service.

25. Can relatives accompany passengers to the boarding gate?
Generally, access beyond designated security checkpoints is restricted to authorised passengers and personnel. Exceptions may be made for approved assistance arrangements.

SECTION 4: ARRIVAL, BAGGAGE AND LOST ITEMS

26. What should I do if my luggage does not arrive?
Immediately report the missing baggage to your airline's baggage services or handling representative before leaving the arrival hall. Obtain a Property Irregularity Report (PIR) or other official reference number.

27. Who is responsible for lost or damaged checked-in luggage?
The airline and its appointed ground-handling company are responsible for handling baggage-related claims in accordance with applicable regulations and contractual conditions.

28. Does FAAN handle passengers' checked-in luggage?
No. Checked-in baggage handling is the responsibility of airlines and their appointed ground-handling companies. FAAN provides and manages airport infrastructure and facilities.

29. What should I do if I lose an item inside the airport terminal?
Report the missing item to the airport's designated Lost and Found office or Customer Service Unit. Provide a description of the item, the location where it was last seen and your contact details.

30. What if I discover that an item is missing from my luggage?
Report the incident immediately to your airline or baggage-handling representative. Where theft is suspected, report it to the appropriate airport security agency.

31. Can I carry liquids, food and personal items on board?
Restrictions depend on the type of item, applicable security regulations and the airline's baggage policy. Passengers should confirm specific requirements with their airline before travelling.

32. What happens if my luggage exceeds the airline's weight limit?
The airline determines applicable excess baggage charges and procedures. Passengers should contact their airline for clarification.

SECTION 5: AIRPORT FACILITIES AND PASSENGER CONVENIENCES

33. Are there restrooms available at the airport?
Yes. Public restrooms are provided at designated locations within airport terminals. Passengers can ask airport information personnel for directions.

34. Are there facilities for passengers with reduced mobility or disabilities?
Airport facilities and assistance arrangements are available to support passengers with reduced mobility. Passengers are encouraged to notify their airlines in advance when special assistance is required.

35. Can elderly passengers and pregnant women receive assistance?
Passengers requiring special assistance should inform their airlines before travelling. They may also approach airport customer service personnel for guidance.

36. Are there medical facilities at the airport?
Medical assistance arrangements are available at airports. Passengers experiencing a medical emergency should immediately notify the nearest airport personnel or security officer.

37. Are there restaurants and shops within the airport?
Yes. Various commercial outlets, restaurants and retail facilities operate within designated airport terminals. Availability varies by airport.

38. Is Wi-Fi available at Nigerian airports?
Wi-Fi availability and access arrangements vary across airports. Passengers should enquire at the airport information desk.

39. Are ATMs and banking services available at the airport?
Selected airports provide banking services and ATM facilities. Availability and operating hours may vary.

40. Can I charge my phone at the airport?
Charging points may be available in designated passenger areas. Passengers should seek assistance from airport information personnel.

41. Are there airport car parking facilities?
Designated parking facilities are available at selected airports. Parking charges, duration, procedures and security arrangements vary by location.

42. Can I leave my vehicle at the airport overnight?
Overnight parking arrangements depend on the airport's approved parking policy and available facilities. Passengers should confirm the applicable fees and conditions before leaving their vehicles.

43. What should I do if my vehicle is damaged or missing in the airport car park?
Report the incident immediately to the car park operator or relevant airport security personnel and obtain an incident report. You may also lodge a complaint with FAAN Customer Service.

SECTION 6: AIRPORT SECURITY, SAFETY AND EMERGENCIES

44. Why are there several security checkpoints at the airport?
Different security agencies and authorised personnel perform specific security responsibilities to ensure the safety of passengers, aircraft, airport workers and facilities.

45. What should I do if I notice suspicious activity at the airport?
Immediately report the incident to the nearest airport security officer, police officer or authorised airport personnel. Do not attempt to investigate suspicious objects or activities yourself.

46. What should I do if I lose my way inside the airport?
Approach the nearest information desk, Customer Service representative or uniformed airport personnel for directions.

47. What should I do in the event of a fire or other emergency?
Remain calm, follow emergency instructions and evacuation signs, and comply with directions from authorised emergency personnel.

48. Can I take photographs or videos inside the airport?
Photography and filming may be restricted in certain operational, security-sensitive and restricted areas. Passengers should comply with airport signage and obtain the necessary permission where required.

49. Why are vehicles restricted from certain areas of the airport?
Traffic and vehicle access restrictions are implemented to ensure passenger safety, maintain orderly traffic flow and protect airport operations.

50. What should I do if an airport staff member demands money from me without justification?
Do not engage in unauthorised payments. Request clarification, note the staff member's identification details where possible, and report the incident to FAAN Customer Service or the appropriate authority.

SECTION 7: PASSENGER RIGHTS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

51. What are my rights as an airport passenger?
Passengers are entitled to:
- Safe and secure airport facilities.
- Respectful and courteous treatment.
- Accurate and timely airport information.
- Access to available airport facilities.
- Assistance when reasonably required.
- An opportunity to submit complaints and receive appropriate feedback.
- Protection against harassment, intimidation and unlawful treatment.

52. What are my responsibilities as a passenger?
Passengers are expected to:
- Comply with airport security procedures.
- Arrive at the airport on time.
- Follow airport signs and instructions.
- Treat airport staff and fellow passengers respectfully.
- Avoid prohibited items and activities.
- Maintain cleanliness within airport facilities.
- Provide accurate travel information.
- Report suspicious activities and safety concerns.

53. What should I do if I am harassed or verbally abused by airport personnel?
Report the incident to FAAN Customer Service. Provide the location, date, approximate time, description of the staff member and any available evidence. Complaints involving other agencies will be referred to the appropriate authority.

54. What should I do if an airport staff member refuses to provide assistance?
Request assistance from a supervisor or the airport Customer Service Unit. You may also submit a formal complaint through the official FAAN Customer Service channels.

55. Can I be charged for using airport facilities?
Some airport services and facilities attract approved charges, while others are provided as part of the airport's general passenger facilities. Passengers should request clarification and official payment information before making payments.

SECTION 8: COMPLAINTS, FEEDBACK AND CUSTOMER SERVICE

56. How do I submit a complaint to FAAN?
Passengers can submit complaints through the official FAAN website's Customer Service page, designated airport Customer Service desks or other officially published FAAN communication channels.

57. What information should I provide when submitting a complaint?
To enable effective investigation, provide:
- Full name and contact details.
- Airport and terminal where the incident occurred.
- Date and approximate time of the incident.
- Flight number, where applicable.
- Description of the complaint.
- Names or identification details of the individuals involved, where available.
- Supporting documents, photographs or other evidence, where applicable.

58. How long does FAAN take to resolve complaints?
FAAN endeavours to acknowledge and investigate complaints as promptly as possible. Resolution timelines depend on the nature, complexity and organisation responsible for the issue. Passengers will be updated where necessary.

59. Can I submit an anonymous complaint?
Yes. However, providing contact details may assist FAAN in obtaining further information and communicating investigation outcomes.

60. How can I follow up on a complaint I have submitted?
Use the complaint reference number issued, where available, when contacting the Customer Service Department for an update.

61. What happens when my complaint concerns an airline rather than FAAN?
FAAN will guide the passenger to the appropriate airline or responsible organization. Where necessary, the complaint may be referred to the relevant authority.

62. Can I complain about an airline through FAAN?
Yes. FAAN can receive and appropriately direct passenger feedback and complaints. However, airline-related matters such as ticket refunds, flight cancellations, baggage claims and airline service disputes should primarily be addressed to the airline and, where necessary, escalated to NCAA.

63. What happens after I submit a complaint?
The complaint is reviewed, categorized and directed to the appropriate department, airport unit or responsible organization for necessary investigation and action. Feedback will be provided through the contact details supplied.

64. Can I make suggestions about improving airport services?
Absolutely. FAAN welcomes suggestions, observations and constructive feedback from passengers to improve service delivery and the overall airport experience.

65. How can I commend an airport staff member for excellent service?
Passengers can submit commendations through the Customer Service page, identifying the staff member, airport, date and nature of the excellent service received.

SECTION 9: UNDERSTANDING THE RESPONSIBILITIES OF AVIATION STAKEHOLDERS

This section is particularly important in helping passengers direct complaints to the right organisation.
- FAAN: Airport management, infrastructure, terminal facilities and airport services.
- Airlines: Ticketing, check-in, boarding, flight schedules, flight delays, cancellations and passenger baggage arrangements.
- NCAA: Regulation and oversight of civil aviation and consumer protection in the aviation industry.
- Airport security agencies: Security screening, access control and other assigned security responsibilities.
- Immigration Service: Passport control, immigration clearance and border control.
- Nigeria Customs Service: Customs control and enforcement of applicable import and export requirements.
- Ground-handling companies: Baggage handling, aircraft ground support and other contracted ground services.
- Federal Airports Authority's concessionaires: Delivery of specific commercial or support services under their respective concession agreements.

66. Does FAAN regulate airlines?
No. FAAN is responsible for airport management and operations, while NCAA is the aviation regulatory authority responsible for regulating airlines and other civil aviation service providers.

67. Does FAAN handle airline baggage complaints?
No. Airlines and their appointed ground handlers are responsible for checked-in baggage handling and related claims.

68. Does FAAN manage all airport terminals in Nigeria?
No. Some terminals are privately managed or operated under concession arrangements. For example, Murtala Muhammed Airport Terminal Two (MM2) is privately managed and is not operated by FAAN. Passengers should direct terminal-specific complaints to the appropriate terminal operator.

69. Who should I contact if I have a complaint about immigration or customs officers?
Complaints involving immigration or customs personnel should be directed to the relevant agency. FAAN Customer Service may provide guidance on the appropriate reporting channel.

70. Who should I contact about an airline's conduct or violation of passenger rights?
Passengers should first contact the airline. If the matter remains unresolved or concerns a breach of applicable aviation regulations or consumer rights, it may be escalated to NCAA.

SECTION 10: SPECIAL PASSENGER SERVICES

71. What assistance is available for passengers with disabilities?
Passengers with disabilities can request appropriate assistance through their airline and airport service personnel. Assistance may include wheelchair support, guidance through terminal facilities and boarding assistance.

72. Can children travel alone?
Some airlines provide unaccompanied minor services subject to age restrictions, documentation and applicable charges. Parents or guardians should contact the airline before booking.

73. What should I do if I am travelling with an infant?
Contact your airline for information on infant fares, baggage allowances, baby strollers, bassinets and other travel requirements.

74. Can I travel with pets?
Pet transportation is subject to airline policies, destination-country regulations and applicable veterinary and animal health requirements. Passengers should obtain approval from their airline before travelling.

75. What should I do if I require urgent medical attention at the airport?
Immediately alert the nearest airport personnel or request emergency medical assistance.

76. Where can I get information about connecting flights?
Passengers should contact their airline for information about connecting flights, transfer procedures, baggage arrangements and minimum connection times.

77. What should I do if I lose my passport at the airport?
Immediately report the loss to the appropriate airport security agency or police and contact the relevant immigration authorities. International passengers should also contact their country's embassy or consulate where necessary.

SECTION 11: AIRPORT TRANSPORTATION AND ACCESS

78. Are taxis available at Nigerian airports?
Taxi and other approved transportation services are available at selected airports. Passengers are encouraged to use authorized transport operators and agree on applicable fares before commencing their journeys.

79. Can private vehicles pick up passengers at the terminal?
Passenger pickup arrangements depend on the airport's traffic management and designated pickup policies. Motorists must comply with airport traffic signs and instructions.

80. What should I do if I experience extortion by transport operators or individuals at the airport?
Report the incident to FAAN Customer Service or the appropriate airport security personnel. Provide the location, time and available details of the individuals involved.

81. Are ride-hailing services permitted at airports?
Access and pickup arrangements for ride-hailing operators vary by airport. Passengers should follow designated pickup points and airport instructions.

SECTION 12: ADDITIONAL PASSENGER ENQUIRIES

82. Where can I find information about airport charges?
Information on approved airport charges and applicable commercial service fees can be obtained from the relevant airport office or service provider.

83. Can I organise an event or commercial activity inside the airport?
Commercial activities, promotions, filming and events within airport premises require appropriate authorisation. Interested individuals or organisations should contact FAAN through its official channels.

84. How can I report a dirty or poorly maintained airport facility?
Submit a complaint through the FAAN Customer Service page, specifying the airport, terminal, exact location and nature of the problem.

85. How can I report a malfunctioning escalator, elevator, air conditioner or airport equipment?
Report the issue immediately to airport Customer Service or the nearest airport personnel. Provide the exact location and description of the fault.

86. How can I report damaged passenger seats or inadequate seating at boarding gates?
Contact FAAN Customer Service and identify the affected terminal and boarding gate so that the matter can be directed to the appropriate unit.

87. Can passengers request assistance with airport directions or terminal transfers?
Yes. Passengers can approach airport information desks or Customer Service personnel for directions and information about available transfer arrangements.

88. How can I participate in FAAN customer satisfaction surveys?
Passengers can participate in surveys and feedback exercises published through official FAAN communication channels and airport Customer Service platforms.
